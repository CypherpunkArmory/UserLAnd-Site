This policy describes what UserLAnd Technologies, LLC ("we") collects when you use our Android apps
(UserLAnd, its standalone Linux apps and our game apps) and UserLAnd Cloud, why, and what you can do
about it.

## The apps

Our apps run on your device. The Linux environments they set up, and everything you do and store
in them, stay on your device; we don't collect them.

- **Crash reports.** If you agree when Google Play asks, Google Play sends us reports when an app
  crashes or stops responding: the device model, Android version and the technical details of the
  crash. We use them only to fix problems. We don't use any other crash-reporting or analytics
  service.
- **Purchases.** Purchases and subscriptions in our apps are made through Google Play. Google
  handles the payment; we receive a record of what was bought, never your payment details.
- **Downloads.** The apps download Linux distributions, packages and, for the game apps, games
  from third-party servers (for example GitHub, the distributions' package mirrors and the Internet
  Archive). Those servers see your IP address, as for any download, under their own policies.

## UserLAnd Cloud

UserLAnd Cloud runs your Linux environment on our servers instead of your device. To provide it we
collect:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your Google account's email address and account ID, from signing in with Google | To identify your account |
| Each device's name (its make and model) and the public half of an SSH key the app creates on it | So only your devices can reach your machines |
| Your cloud machines: their files, the software you install, their settings and passwords | It is the service; see below |
| How long your machines run each month | Your plan's hours |
| Your UserLAnd Cloud subscriptions: the Google Play purchase record, plan, state and renewal date | To give you the plan you paid for |
| IP addresses and times of connections to our servers, in logs | To run the service, keep it secure and fix problems |

**Your machines' contents.** Everything on a cloud machine is stored on our servers. We don't look
at it, except as needed to keep the service running, to investigate abuse of these terms (such as
attacks on others or mass mailing), or when required by law. Connections to UserLAnd Cloud are
encrypted (HTTPS and SSH). Your machine's disk is not separately encrypted on our servers, so don't
keep anything on it that you couldn't afford to have exposed if our servers were compromised.

**Where.** UserLAnd Cloud runs on servers rented from OVHcloud in data centres in the United
States. Your data is stored and processed there.

**Who else is involved.** OVHcloud provides the servers. Google provides sign-in, Google Play
billing and the notifications Play sends us about your subscriptions. They process the data
involved on our behalf or as described in their own policies. We don't sell your information,
and we don't share it with anyone else unless required by law.

**How long we keep it.**

- Your account and everything in it, until you delete it, or until we close it under the terms.
- A cloud machine and its files, until you delete the machine or your account. If you no longer
  have a subscription or trial time, we may delete your machines after warning you in the app;
  see the [terms](/terms/).
- Server logs, 30 days.
- Backups of our account database (which doesn't include your machines' files), 14 days.
- After you delete your account we keep only a one-way hash of your Google account ID together
  with the free trial time it used, so a new sign-up doesn't get a second trial. It can't be
  turned back into your account or email.

## Deleting your data

- **Your UserLAnd Cloud account:** in the UserLAnd app, open the menu → UserLAnd Cloud account →
  Delete account, or go to [cloud.userland.tech/delete-account](https://cloud.userland.tech/delete-account)
  and sign in with the same Google account. Your machines and their files, devices, usage and
  purchase records are deleted at once. Any UserLAnd Cloud subscription is cancelled, and Google
  Play refunds the unused part of the current period. Uninstalling the app doesn't delete the
  account.
- **Anything else,** or a copy of what we hold about you: email
  [support@userland.tech](mailto:support@userland.tech).

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California) you have further rights,
such as to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to how we use it.
Email us to use them. We don't use your data for advertising and we don't sell it.

## Children

Our apps and UserLAnd Cloud are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect
personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will
delete it.

## Changes

We may revise this policy from time to time and will post the current version here. If a
revision meaningfully reduces your rights, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes
effect.
